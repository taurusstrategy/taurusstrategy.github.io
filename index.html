Meet the Founder - Katarzyna Brzozowska

Welcome! My name is Kate.
And I never wanted to build another consulting company.

I wanted to understand why some people create businesses that give them freedom... while others become prisoners of the businesses they built.

Most business advice focuses on strategy. Most personal development focuses on mindset. Very few people understand that sustainable success requires both. Your business can never consistently outperform the person running it.

For years I worked in AI, cybersecurity and business transformation, helping organisations solve complex technological problems. At the same time I became fascinated by something completely different. Why brilliant people keep repeating the same patterns. Why successful founders feel burned out and depressed. Why financial success often creates new problems instead of freedom. How one can build a sucessful, abundant life and hapiness.

Into psychology. Subconcious reprogramming. Behavioural science. Investing and money systems. Technology. Neuroscience. Hidden human potential.

Today I combine both worlds. The analytical and the deeply human. Because I no longer believe business problems are only business problems. Sometimes they are identity problems. Sometimes decision-making problems. Sometimes emotional regulation problems. Sometimes systems problems. Sometimes they simply require better strategy.

My philosophy

Your business will never consistently outperform the person leading it.

Most people try to fix individual business problems. I believe lasting change begins with understanding the entire system behind them.

Your business is not separate from your decisions. Your decisions are not separate from your beliefs, emotional patterns, nervous system, habits and relationship with money.

The technology you choose, the way you communicate, the opportunities you pursue and the risks you avoid are all influenced by the person you have become and the future you believe is possible for you.

That is why Taurus Strategy connects disciplines that are usually treated as separate: business architecture, artificial intelligence, the future of money, behavioural psychology, leadership, somatic practices, meditation, shadow work and other practical systems for building and growing you as an owner and your modern business.

Depending on what you need, we may work on your strategy, business model, financial direction, personal brand, digital systems, use of AI or mental mastery to remove unconscious blocks that may slow down your business expansion. We can work on identifying the patterns, fears and internal blocks that continue to influence your decisions.

I do not believe in forcing people into someone else’s formula for success. The goal is one: to help you remove what limits you, strengthen your ability to lead and build a business that is aligned with your ambitions, values and desired way of living.

We want to see you winning.
Because every person who builds a fulfilling and successful life gives others permission to believe they can too.
