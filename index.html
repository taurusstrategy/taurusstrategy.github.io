Meet the Founder — Katarzyna Brzozowska

I work where technology, finance and security meet.
And I built Taurus Strategy around that intersection.

My background is in engineering, cloud and cybersecurity. Today, my work increasingly focuses on artificial intelligence: how organisations adopt it, secure it, govern it and understand its economics.

At the same time, I study Investment Banking at SGH Warsaw School of Economics and research how AI, blockchain, tokenisation and digital money may reshape banking, investing and financial infrastructure.

Taurus Strategy brings these worlds together: deep technology, security and finance.

I focus on questions that increasingly sit between traditional organisational boundaries:

→ Where does AI create measurable value in financial services?
→ How do we control and optimise the growing cost of AI?
→ What happens when AI agents can initiate or influence financial actions?
→ How should digital money and emerging financial infrastructure be secured?

These are not separate technology questions. They affect economics, operating models, security, regulation and the way financial products are designed.

My work is about understanding those connections and translating them into practical business decisions.

The future cannot be predicted perfectly.
But it can be understood early enough to prepare for it.

Taurus Strategy combines independent research with strategic and technical advisory.

I help organisations separate structural change from hype, understand the economics and risks behind new technology, and decide what deserves investment, experimentation or stronger controls.

The focus is not on predicting every trend. It is on making better decisions before the implications become obvious.

Why Taurus Strategy

Technology doesn't change finance in isolation. Everything around it changes too.

AI changes economics, operating models and decision-making. Digital money changes financial infrastructure. New systems create new attack surfaces, dependencies and regulatory questions.

Looking at only one part of that system makes it easy to miss what is actually happening.

Taurus Strategy looks across AI, finance, security and economics to understand how those forces interact.

That means asking practical questions: What will AI cost at scale? Where does automation create new risk? How could new forms of digital money change products and infrastructure? Which changes matter now, and which can wait?

That is why Taurus Strategy connects disciplines that are often treated separately: artificial intelligence, AI economics, cybersecurity, financial technology, digital money and the future of financial infrastructure.

The goal is not to follow every technology trend. It is to understand the changes with material implications for your organisation and turn them into better strategic, technical and economic decisions.
